Frames × Loadout

Rich's question: now the loadout makes every day varied, does that change Wednesday and Friday? Yes — it frees them to become what events are actually for.

The insight

The events were doing two jobs at once — worth pulling apart:

  • Variety — "today ≠ yesterday." The loadout now owns this.
  • Occasion — "today is a landmark: purpose, stakes, emotion." This is what events are for.

Variety is spice; occasion is a landmark. The tell that the old events were mis-defined — "both are just different ways of doing MC" — is that they were named after a legacy mechanic, not their essence. Battleground's essence is claim your weak spots, no-lose, end-of-week redemption; MC was incidental.

Frame

Event frames are special frames; the loadout fills them — with the FULL deck now, not just MC. Events don't compete with the loadout; the loadout powers them.

Wednesday & Friday become frame slots that different frames rotate through over a term — so even the events stay fresh. (Realises SEASONS' "the event loadout is a chapter-level variable.")

Battleground, reimagined

Frame unchanged: 4 claimable zones on the kid's weak topics, no-lose, territory bar, "% claimed." The loadout now fills each zone with the best mechanic for that topic — each zone wearing that mechanic's signature colour, so the board reads at a glance:

The weak topic is…Claim it by…
a vocab / key termtyping it — Short-text
a sequence / timeline / methodordering it — Ordering
a maths methodsolving it — Numeric
a true/false-able factswiping it — Swipe
a plain fact / conceptpicking it — MC / spot-the-lie

"Claim your ground" becomes a varied, multi-gesture redemption run — far richer than four MC. The teach-back finishing move stays (explain the topic you just reclaimed).

Blitz, reimagined

  • Frame = tempo / energy: fast, high-score, midweek. Filled with fast mechanics (rapid swipes, quick MC, mental Numeric) — "a fast day," not "an MC day."
  • Reversed becomes a card in the deck — featured on Blitz day, but free to appear occasionally elsewhere. Decoupled from Wednesday.

Bonus — this fixes the naming trap: Wed/Fri are frame slots; the frame in each can rotate. No more "boss directive that produces Battleground."

The risk to hold onto: events derive power from contrast. Now the baseline is exciting, an event must be a real step-change (higher stakes / distinct frame / bigger reward), or it blends into "another varied day." Keep normal days a comfortable daily run so events stand out.
