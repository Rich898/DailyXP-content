# Absence & streaks — design principle

*"Life happens." A learning product that treats a missed day as failure gets deleted. Absence must be NEUTRAL, never punitive. How the system handles sick days / holidays / rough weeks is part of its character — and a genuine selling point vs guilt-trip competitors.*

## Cases to design for
- **One-off missed day** — quiz waits or rolls forward. No pile-up, no "you failed." Streak must NOT break for a single normal-life gap (grace days / streak-freeze / rest-days-don't-count). Duolingo-style streak punishment is demotivating for a study tool — avoid.
- **Planned/known absence** (sick week, holidays, exam leave) — a parent/kid "pause me" toggle: quizzes hold, nothing accrues as debt, clean resume, no guilt, no wall of unplayed quizzes.
- **Return = fresh start, not backlog.** Spaced-repetition advantage: you DON'T replay missed days. The ledger already knows what's untested and resurfaces those topics naturally in the next live quiz. Missing days creates no debt — those topics are simply still "due." Structural edge over fixed-syllabus tools (Tassomai etc.).
- **Don't nag the sick kid.** Absence suppresses daily soundbytes (silence is correct). Friday summary frames a gap as context ("quieter week — was off sick"), never a red metric ("completion down 60%").

## Mechanics (build in scheduler / v3.1 era)
- Pause-mode (per student, parent- or self-set); auto-resume on a date or manual.
- Streak model that survives normal-life gaps.
- Absence-aware reporting (suppress dailies; contextualise the weekly).
- Ledger stays frozen during absence — no penalty, trend simply shows the gap honestly.
